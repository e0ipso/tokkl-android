Where the brand images in res/drawable-*/tokkl_wordmark_*.png and
res/drawable-*/tokkl_icon_*.png come from

Sources: the owner's Tokkl artwork in ../tokkl/docs/brand (github.com/e0ipso/tokkl
at 36bcba9), PNG only:
  tokkl-wordmark-v3.png, 1774x887 RGBA, SHA-256
  e3653f852d75bc2786ea5acbef7a3aebd396223c5188a24fde1eaf9195beb7b7
  tokkl-icon-v4-1024.png, 1024x1024 RGBA, SHA-256
  874f7ec9ccb91c4c60b426a4aa75827db1ed96833cdfbcd3b7e24edb3efe3aae
The icon is the launcher icon's source too (res/mipmap-anydpi/ic_launcher.xml). The
launcher layers carry padding for the adaptive icon mask, so the brand icon is cut
from the source again.

Crop: Tokkl's WORDMARK_CROP in ../tokkl/scripts/icons.ts, 1645x709+80+91, the
letters and paint spots inside the source's transparent margin, plus 4 px.

Sizes: ui/Brand.kt draws the icon at 24, 30 or 48 dp beside the wordmark at 84, 130
or 260 dp wide (sm, md and lg, as in Tokkl's Brand.tsx). Each size has its own
image in each density bucket, at 1x (mdpi), 1.5x (hdpi), 2x (xhdpi), 3x (xxhdpi)
and 4x (xxxhdpi), so each size draws from an image made for its width near the
screen's density, never from a larger size scaled down. Widths in px, wordmark
then icon:
  sm  84 126 168 252  336   24 36 48  72  96
  md 130 195 260 390  520   30 45 60  90 120
  lg 260 390 520 780 1040   48 72 96 144 192
The widest, 1040 px, is under the crop's 1645 px, so nothing is upscaled.

Built with ImageMagick 7.1.1-43 with Tokkl's own steps from scripts/icons.ts:
resize, then reduce to a 256-colour palette with alpha, and strip metadata and
timestamps. W is the wordmark width and I the icon size in px for one size and
bucket:
  magick tokkl-wordmark-v3.png -crop 1645x709+80+91 +repage -resize Wx
    -colors 256 -strip -define png:exclude-chunks=date,time
    png:drawable-BUCKET/tokkl_wordmark_SIZE.png
  magick tokkl-icon-v4-1024.png -resize IxI
    -colors 256 -strip -define png:exclude-chunks=date,time
    png:drawable-BUCKET/tokkl_icon_SIZE.png
The 260 and 520 px wordmarks and the 96 px icon come out byte for byte the same as
Tokkl's packages/web/public/brand/wordmark-260.png, wordmark-520.png and
icon-96.png.

This note lives in res/raw because the build accepts only drawable files in
res/drawable-*.
